About

Krishnendu is a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Biomedical Engineering at Pennsylvania State University, working on optical imaging techniques for biomedical studies. He completed his PhD in Physics at IIT Delhi in 2022, with a visiting research stint in the Optical Nanoscopy group at UiT–The Arctic University of Norway.

His work spans both simulation-based analysis and hands-on experimental development, centered on super-resolution microscopy, digital holographic microscopy, photoacoustic microscopy with metalenses, and miniature microscopes for preclinical studies. He contributed to the development of transmission structured illumination microscopy and acousto-optical elastography using a transparent ultrasound transducer, work that led to two international patent filings. In his postdoctoral research, he has extended these techniques toward plasmonic detection of cancer exosomes and holography-based optical elastography, alongside work in photo-lithography, laser speckle contrast imaging, and intrinsic optical sensing.
